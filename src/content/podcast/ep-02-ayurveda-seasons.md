What if your body needed different food in February than it does in July — and the wisdom
for exactly *what* was 3,000 years old? **Dr. Anjali Rao** joins us to unpack *ritucharya*,
Ayurveda's seasonal routine, and turn it into something you can actually cook from.

We talk doshas without the jargon, why digestion is the foundation of everything, and how
to read your own signals — the bloating, the restlessness, the winter heaviness — as
information rather than problems. It's the perfect companion to our
[Eat Seasonally](/eat) hub.

## In this episode

- **(03:20)** Ritucharya: what "seasonal routine" actually means
- **(12:00)** The three doshas in plain language — and how the seasons stir them
- **(21:45)** Agni: why Ayurveda treats digestion as the root of health
- **(31:10)** Spring cleansing without the fads
- **(40:30)** Cooling the fire of summer; grounding the winds of autumn
- **(50:15)** One dish per season Anjali cooks on repeat
